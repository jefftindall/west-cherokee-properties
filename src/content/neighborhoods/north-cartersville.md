10 Falcon Circle is a neighborhood street on the north side of Cartersville, quieter than the square and set up for daily life. The Bartow County College and Career Academy is right across the street. Walmart, QuikTrip, and Lowe's are close enough that errands stay simple.

The Savoy Automobile Museum is a short drive when you want something to do on a weekend. Downtown is a few minutes south if you would rather walk the square for supper.

I-75 is right here — handy for work in Atlanta or a trip north. Our downtown homes have the same interstate access from the other side of town; Falcon Circle is simply the closer on-ramp.
