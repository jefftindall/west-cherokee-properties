# Georgia eviction notice template

**Audience:** Staff preparing a notice to pay rent or quit in `/office`  
**Last updated:** 2026-08-30  
**Runtime copy (Functions):** [`api/src/lib/georgia-eviction-notice-template.md`](../../api/src/lib/georgia-eviction-notice-template.md) — keep these two files identical

Seven-day notice to pay rent or quit for nonpayment, derived from West Cherokee Properties' prior Georgia notices. Not a substitute for attorney review. Do not collect SSN or protected-class fields.

# NOTICE TO PAY RENT OR QUIT

{{notice_date}}

{{tenant_name}}  
{{tenant_mailing_address}}

**TO TENANT(S) AND ALL OTHERS IN POSSESSION OF THE PREMISES LOCATED AT:**

{{premises_address}}

PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that pursuant to the lease and/or rental agreement dated {{lease_date}} under which you hold the possession of the herein described premises there is now due unpaid and delinquent rent in the total sum of **{{amount_due}}**, representing rent due for the period from {{period_start}} to {{period_end}}.

PLEASE TAKE FURTHER NOTICE that within **SEVEN (7) days** after service of this notice, you are hereby required to pay the above-listed amount in full **OR** quit the subject premises, move out, and deliver up possession of the same to {{possession_deliver_to}}. Failure to pay the rent in full **OR** vacate the premises **WITHIN SEVEN (7) days** as required by this notice will result in forfeiture of the lease and/or rental agreement and will institute a dispossessory action against you to recover rent, damages and possession of said premises.

**THIS IS INTENDED AS A SEVEN (7) DAY LEGAL NOTICE FOR THE PURPOSE OF TERMINATING YOUR TENANCY. THIS NOTICE IS IN ACCORDANCE WITH GEORGIA STATE PROPERTY CODE.**
